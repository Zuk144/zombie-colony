- ★★★ Tree plots and wild trees re-seeding near other trees. [owner playtest; Banished forester]
- ★★★ Zombies drop what they carried: scrap, cloth, ammo, and sometimes medicine or components. [DESIGN §8.2; Zomboid]
- ★★★ The dead dress for what they carry: uniforms read at a glance (police blue means rounds, scrubs mean medicine, overalls mean tools), so you pick your noise by who's coming. Lead idea for the "What the dead carried" brief. [vision one-pager; owner's item-depth taste]

- ★★ Every item has two uses (a crowbar is a weapon and a faster salvage tool; cloth is bandages or clothes; a car battery powers a floodlight or becomes components). [vision one-pager]
- ★★★ The Din as a resource dial: loud to harvest, quiet to survive. [DESIGN §8.2]
- ★★★ Named districts to clear and fence (pharmacy, police, hardware, gas station) with perks. [DESIGN §8.3; They Are Billions villages]
- ★★ Components as the progression gate instead of a research tree. [references/rimworld.md]
- ★★ Variety drives upgrades: rooms or survivors want *different* goods, not more. [Manor Lords]

## Threat and the Director

- ★ A "sally out" policy lets fighters leave the wire to engage (the default is to hold it). [supply-line slice]

## People and society
- ★★★ Policies as indirect control, each with a visible cost: curfew, lockdown, rations, double shifts, lights out. [Prison Architect regime; Songs of Syx decrees]
- ★★ Survivors make demands (a day off, better food); refusing costs mood. [Prison Architect]
- ★★ Luxuries as a scavenging goal and mood lever: coffee, cigarettes, liquor, "the last jar of coffee". [Norland beer]
- ★★ Key roles that make people precious: a Leader trait needed to run an expedition or the radio tower. [Norland nobles]
- ★★ Factions inside the colony (the faithful, the pragmatists, the newcomers), with choice events that please one and upset another. [Norland]
- ★★ Mood memories that fade over about 90 days at half strength. [Manor Lords approval]
- ★ Strange-mood equivalent: a survivor obsessed with fixing the old radio demands specific parts. [Dwarf Fortress]
- ★ A desperate survivor steals food; the colony's justice is a policy. [Norland crime]
- ★★★ Every survivor arrives tied to one other person (a sibling, an old debt, a stranger they don't trust), so a bite or a turn lands on someone. [CDO first play 2026-09-29: the untouched seed-11 colony lost all three with no one grieving anyone; The Walking Dead]
- ★★ The turned come home: a turned survivor drifts back toward their old bed or the gate, so "Zeke's rattling the fence" is a 3am moment. The name label already exists. [CDO first play; The Walking Dead]
- ★★ Grief refuses the burn pit: a survivor won't let their loved one's body be burned and sits with it until it rises. [direction brainstorm; The Walking Dead]
- ★★ Rename RimWorld-verbatim thoughts ("Extremely low expectations") to zombie-world ones ("Still breathing"). [CDO first play; clone trap]
